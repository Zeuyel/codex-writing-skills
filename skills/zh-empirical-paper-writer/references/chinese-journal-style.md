# Chinese Journal Style

Use for `prose-drafting` and `prose-revision`.

1. Drafting into TeX

## 1. Drafting Into TeX

Draft or revise directly in the target `.tex` file when the task is formal
manuscript writing. Do not prepare a Markdown正文草稿 first.

For a paragraph replacement, keep the result as one continuous Chinese paragraph
in TeX source. Do not split the paragraph into sentence-per-line drafts.

Preserve the paper argument, variables, equations, citations, labels, and
identification logic unless the user asks for substantive changes.

## 2. Structural Order

Do not begin with wordsmithing. Inspect and repair in this order:

1. chapter or section task
2. paragraph identity and landing
3. sentence movement and sentence obligations
4. vocabulary, collocation, aspect marking, and rhythm
5. anti-AI and anti-translationese pass

Default movement for formal Chinese journal prose:

1. locate the object or problem under discussion
2. state the local claim or task early enough
3. unfold the mechanism, evidence, or literature handling
4. land on a concrete implication or handoff

Avoid slogan-like significance, broad era narration, self-congratulatory
contribution claims, and endings such as `具有重要意义` unless a concrete
analytical claim follows.

For chapter openings, section openings, and section closings, prefer 3-5 shorter
sentences over one long sentence with multiple subordinate clauses.

## 3. Sentence-Role Framework

Use `The Craft of Research` as the upper-level movement control.

Macro obligations:

1. `context`: locate the discussion
2. `problem`: show what is missing, difficult, inconsistent, or unresolved
3. `response`: state what this paper, section, or paragraph does next

Argument obligations:

1. `claim`: what is being asserted
2. `reason`: why the claim is plausible
3. `evidence`: what literature, institutional, data, or model fact supports it
4. `warrant`: why that evidence matters for this claim

Sentence roles and obligations:

- `权威启动句`: borrow relevant authority; do not decorate.
- `背景定位句`: narrow the domain; do not sprawl.
- `问题提出句`: identify a concrete unresolved tension.
- `观点句`: make the local point visible early.
- `承接句`: carry forward real old information.
- `展开句`: advance one mechanism or distinction.
- `例证句`: support an already stated point.
- `warrant句`: explain why the evidence is relevant.
- `收束句`: extract the local conclusion.
- `过渡句`: hand off one unresolved aspect or next analytical step.

If a sentence has no lawful role, delete, split, or reassign it.

## 4. Anti-Translationese

Repair these before accepting a sentence:

1. abstract nouns doing verb work, such as `……的提高`, `……的实现`, or `……的转化`
2. weak verb shells, such as `进行研究`, `产生影响`, `发挥作用`
3. prepositional scaffolding that delays the object, such as `对于……而言`,
   `关于……的问题`, `基于……`, or `通过……来`
4. English-style category-first openings, such as `第一类文献` or `三条研究线索`
5. passive or causative shells that hide the actual actor
6. overlong sentences carrying multiple unrelated obligations
7. repeated `不是……而是……` and `一方面……另一方面……` without a real contrast
8. repeated fillers such as `需要指出的是`, `由此可见`, `这意味着`

Default repair sequence:

1. name the concrete economic or organizational object
2. choose one strong predicate
3. turn nominalized shells into event clauses when possible
4. delete empty setup phrases
5. split overloaded sentences
6. reread for Chinese rhythm

## 5. Material-Bank And Collocation Discipline

Use two material layers:

1. the general bank below for common nouns, verbs, collocations, and connectors
2. the project bank when available, usually
   `research-notes/writing/material-banks/project_material_bank.md`

Default loading order:

1. check the general bank
2. check the project bank for project-specific wording
3. check designated Chinese reference articles when provided
4. then draft or revise the sentence

Do not improvise a low-frequency collocation if it is absent from the general
bank, project bank, and reference register. Use a simpler native expression or
mark it for later supplementation.

### Specialized-Term Provenance

Specialized terms are allowed only when they are traceable.

For the first manuscript use of a term such as `技术轨道`, `技术空间`,
`边界搜索`, `能力边界`, `组织验证能力`, or `升级陷阱`, the prose or nearby
literature setup must make clear:

1. which article, theory stream, or project theorem introduced or supports the
   term;
2. what the term means in plain firm-level behavior;
3. what this manuscript will observe in data for that term.

If no source article or project theorem can be named, replace the term with
ordinary object-language, such as `企业是否进入过去没有申请过专利的技术类别`.

Do not use abstract nouns as private shorthand. Readers should not need to infer
the author's hidden concept map.

### Connectors And Adverbs

Use connectors only when the rhetorical move matches:

- 转折: `然而`, `但是`
- 举例: `例如`
- 递进: `进一步`, `此外`, `进一步分析发现`
- 并列补充: `同时`
- 因果收束: `因此`, `基于此`, `基于以上分析`
- 细化说明: `具体地`

High-risk adverbs and fillers:

- `更加`
- `更为`
- `尤为`
- `显然`
- `实际上`
- `本质上`
- `某种程度上`
- `在很大程度上`
- `系统性地` when no system is shown
- `全面地` when no scope is specified

## 6. Reference-Constrained Wording

When a designated Chinese reference article exists:

1. extract its actual connectors
2. extract its actual adverb-verb and verb-object combinations
3. compare the target passage against that inventory
4. replace writer-side meta verbs first
5. replace inflated verbs second
6. polish sentence rhythm last

Writer-side meta verbs usually belong in notes, not manuscript body:

- `收束`
- `压缩`
- `固定`
- `拉回`
- `写成`
- `锚定`
- `统摄`
- `装配`
- `回填`
- `对齐`

Use inflated verbs cautiously:

- `重塑`
- `赋能`
- `激发`
- `深化`
- `重构`
- `塑造`
- `驱动`

They are acceptable only when the object is concrete and a mechanism follows.

When writing what existing studies have already done, check aspect marking.
Prefer completed forms such as `展开了讨论`, `积累了成果`, `形成了共识`, and
`转向了机制分析` when the sentence expresses completed accumulation, shift, or
prior handling.

## 7. Terms, Deletion, And Output

Preferred terminology:

- `empirical` -> `实证上`, `实证层面`, or `实证部分`
- `theory-driven empirical paper` -> `理论驱动的实证研究`
- `regime` -> `区间`, `状态区间`, or `组织区间`
- `baseline regression` -> `基准回归`
- `mechanism analysis` -> `机制检验` or `机制分析`
- `robustness` -> `稳健性检验` or `稳健性分析`

Delete or compress unless directly part of the paper's contribution:

- proof-assistant or Lean4 workflow
- compilation environment and software setup
- coding or verification pipeline
- prompt design process
- data cleaning scripts and implementation details
- long self-praise about contribution
- generic significance claims

After drafting or revising manuscript prose, load `post-draft-audit.md` and run
an audit pass before treating the text as final.
