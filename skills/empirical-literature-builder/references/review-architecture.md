# Review Architecture

## Goal

Write literature reviews for empirical papers in a way that gives readers a clean map of:

1. what has been studied
2. how it has been studied
3. how the methods and views evolved
4. what still matters for the current paper

## Default organization

Use `主题、观点、方法或者机制` as the organizing unit.

A strong default order is:

1. research theme
2. core viewpoints or findings
3. representative methods or measurement approaches
4. remaining unresolved issue
5. bridge to the next theme or to the paper's question

## Paragraph template

A good block often follows this movement:

- first sentence: define the problem or theme
- middle sentences: summarize findings and methods together
- last sentence: land on what is still unresolved and why that matters

## Transition to research gap

Do not jump directly from literature list to `研究缺口`.
Use an intermediate sentence that summarizes the evolution first.

Recommended movement:

- `整体看，现有研究已经从……推进到……。`
- `但在……这一层面，相关讨论仍然不够充分。`
- `这意味着，后续研究需要进一步回答……。`

## Transition to research value

After the gap, move to value in two steps:

1. explain what the paper will add to the unresolved problem
2. explain why that addition matters for identification, mechanism, or interpretation

Avoid empty claims such as `具有重要理论意义和现实意义` unless they are followed by a concrete statement.
