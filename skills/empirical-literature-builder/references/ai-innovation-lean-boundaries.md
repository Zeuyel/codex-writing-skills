## Core positioning

- The paper is a `理论驱动的实证文章`.
- The theory section exists to fix research objects, mechanism chains, boundary conditions, and hypotheses for later empirical work.

## Current drafting boundary

- Chapters 1 and 2 are the current formal writing scope.
- Chapters 3 and later must not be written as formal manuscript text until literature, variables, data, and identification details are fixed by authority materials.

## Writing prohibitions

- turn the paper into a pure theory paper
- generate later empirical chapters from model imagination alone
- write unsupported novelty or gap claims
- write Lean4, compilation, or workflow details into the manuscript body
